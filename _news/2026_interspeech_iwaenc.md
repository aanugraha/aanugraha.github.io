Our team had three papers in September 2026, one at Interspeech 2026 in Sydney and two at IWAENC 2026 in Cremona. These works cover speech separation guided by source directions, and joint audio-visual analysis of indoor scenes with distributed sensors.

- "[Fast Multichannel Nonnegative Matrix Factorization with Directivity Regularization for DOA-Informed Speech Separation](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2026-2139)," Ryosuke Ono, Aditya Arie Nugraha, Yoshiaki Bando, Kazuyoshi Yoshii. (Interspeech 2026)

- "Joint Separation and Tracking of Moving Speakers Based on a Unified Audio-visual Probabilistic Model for Distributed Sensors," Ryunosuke Nihei, Yoshiaki Bando, Aditya Arie Nugraha, Yosuke Ito, Kazuyoshi Yoshii. (IWAENC 2026)

- "Joint Sound Source Separation and 3D Shape Estimation Based on Multimodal Gaussian Primitives," Haruaki Asano, Yoshiaki Bando, Aditya Arie Nugraha, Yosuke Ito, Kazuyoshi Yoshii. (IWAENC 2026)

Many thanks to all collaborators!
