In November 2026, I co-authored four papers at two conferences.

At ACM Multimedia 2026 in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, our demo paper described live guitar tablature transcription, which estimates the string and fret of each played note from microphone audio:

- "[Mic to Fretboard: Live Guitar Tablature Transcription](https://doi.org/10.1145/3767308.3839004)," Aditya Arie Nugraha, Nami Iino, Kazuyoshi Yoshii, Masatoshi Hamanaka.

At APSIPA ASC 2026 in Bangkok, Thailand, students at Kyoto University presented three papers on room acoustics, head-related transfer functions, and unsupervised speech separation:

- "Joint Estimation of Source Position and Room Acoustics Based on the Differentiable Image Source Model," Kotaro Ozawa, Aditya Arie Nugraha, Yosuke Ito, Kazuyoshi Yoshii.

- "Joint Estimation of Head-Related and Room Transfer Functions Based on the Differentiable Image Source Model," Kazuma Ohgo, Diego Di Carlo, Hiroyuki Ueda, Aditya Arie Nugraha, Yosuke Ito, Kazuyoshi Yoshii.

- "Local Gaussian Diffusion Posterior Sampling for Unsupervised Multichannel Noisy Speech Separation," Zimo Zhang, Aditya Arie Nugraha, Kazuyoshi Yoshii, Tatsuya Kawahara.

Many thanks to all collaborators!
