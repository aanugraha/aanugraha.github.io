I am a research scientist in the <a href="https://www.riken.jp/en/research/labs/aip/domain/music_inf_intell/index.html">Music Information Intelligence Team</a> at the <a href="https://www.riken.jp/en/research/labs/aip/index.html">RIKEN Center for Advanced Intelligence Project (RIKEN-AIP)</a> and a visiting researcher in the <a href="https://sts.kuee.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/">Spatio-Temporal Sensing Laboratory</a> at <a href="https://www.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/">Kyoto University</a>.

Before joining the Music Information Intelligence Team in April 2026, I was a member of the <a href="https://aip.riken.jp/labs/domain_intel/sound_scene_understand/">Sound Scene Understanding Team</a> at RIKEN-AIP until its closing in March 2026.

I received a Doctorate in Informatics from the <a href="http://iaem.univ-lorraine.fr/">University of Lorraine</a>, France, for my doctoral research on deep neural network-based multichannel audio source separation. The research was conducted at <a href="https://team.inria.fr/multispeech/">Inria Nancy -- Grand-Est</a>, France, under the supervision of <a href="https://members.loria.fr/EVincent/">Dr. Emmanuel Vincent</a> and <a href="https://members.loria.fr/ALiutkus/">Dr. Antoine Liutkus</a>. My <a href="https://www.theses.fr/en/2017LORR0212">doctoral thesis</a> covered applications of source separation to speech enhancement, singing voice separation, musical instrument separation, and noise-robust speech recognition.

My current research interests include <i>audio source separation</i>, <i>speech enhancement</i>, <i>spatial audio</i>, <i>audio-visual scene understanding</i>, and <i>machine learning</i>, with a particular interest in probabilistic modeling, deep learning, and physics-informed approaches to audio intelligence.
